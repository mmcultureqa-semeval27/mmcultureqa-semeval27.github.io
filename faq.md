<section class="faq-section" id="participation" markdown="1">
## Participation

<details class="faq-item" open markdown="1">
<summary>Who can participate in MMCultureQA at SemEval-2027?</summary>

Anyone can participate, either independently or as part of a team.
</details>

<details class="faq-item" markdown="1">
<summary>Is there a fee to participate?</summary>

Participation in the shared task is completely free.
</details>

<details class="faq-item" markdown="1">
<summary>How can I sign up?</summary>

Fill in the [team registration form]({{ site.registration_url }}), download the data from the [Hugging Face dataset]({{ site.dataset_url }}), and join our [community Slack]({{ site.slack_url }}) for announcements and updates. See the [participation page]({{ '/participate/' | relative_url }}) for the submission workflow.
</details>

<details class="faq-item" markdown="1">
<summary>Can I participate in both MMCultureQA tasks?</summary>

Yes. Teams may participate in Task 1 (Spoken Visual QA), Task 2 (Textual Visual QA), or both, in any subset of the available language tracks. See the [task definitions]({{ '/tasks/' | relative_url }}) for details.
</details>

<details class="faq-item" markdown="1">
<summary>Can I participate in multiple teams for the same task?</summary>

This depends on the task policy. If this is relevant to your team, contact the organizers before submitting runs.
</details>

<details class="faq-item" markdown="1">
<summary>Which models and methods can I use?</summary>

Pretrained models and public external data are permitted; document what you use in your system paper. Follow the [participation rules]({{ '/participate/#rules' | relative_url }}) and any additional limits announced by the organizers.
</details>
</section>

<section class="faq-section" id="evaluation-and-data" markdown="1">
## Evaluation and Data

<details class="faq-item" markdown="1">
<summary>What does the evaluation period mean?</summary>

The evaluation period is the official competition window. Organizers release evaluation data at the beginning of this period, and system outputs are due before the deadline.
</details>

<details class="faq-item" markdown="1">
<summary>How many runs can a team submit?</summary>

Submission limits are task-specific. Check the task page and platform instructions for the final limits.
</details>

<details class="faq-item" markdown="1">
<summary>Where can I find the datasets?</summary>

The data is available from the [MMCultureQA dataset on Hugging Face]({{ site.dataset_url }}). See the [dataset page]({{ '/oasis/' | relative_url }}) and official announcements for release details.
</details>
</section>

<section class="faq-section" id="paper-requirements" markdown="1">
## Paper Requirements

<details class="faq-item" open markdown="1">
<summary>Are all participating teams eligible to submit a system description paper?</summary>

Yes. All participating teams are eligible to submit a system description paper. Acceptance is subject to peer review and compliance with SemEval submission guidelines.
</details>

<details class="faq-item" markdown="1">
<summary>What is the paper format?</summary>

System papers should follow the official SemEval-2027 submission guidelines, including the required template and page limits. Check organizer announcements for submission instructions and deadlines.
</details>

<details class="faq-item" markdown="1">
<summary>How should the paper title be formatted?</summary>

Use this format:

`<Team Name> at SemEval-2027 <Task Name>: <Your Contribution>`

Example:

`YourTeam at SemEval-2027 MMCultureQA: Culturally Grounded Visual Question Answering`

Follow any additional title requirements in the official SemEval-2027 submission guidelines.
</details>

<details class="faq-item" markdown="1">
<summary>Must I cite the task overview and dataset papers?</summary>

Yes. Each team is required to cite the shared task overview and dataset papers in its system paper.
</details>

<details class="faq-item" markdown="1">
<summary>What happens if I exceed the page limit?</summary>

Submissions that exceed length requirements may be desk rejected.
</details>
</section>

<section class="faq-section" id="review-and-publication" markdown="1">
## Review and Publication

<details class="faq-item" markdown="1">
<summary>Where will accepted system description papers be published, and will they be indexed?</summary>

Accepted system description papers will be published in the official SemEval-2027 proceedings through the ACL Anthology. Previous SemEval proceedings have been indexed in Scopus, but we cannot guarantee indexing for 2027 at this stage.
</details>

<details class="faq-item" markdown="1">
<summary>Are there fees for submitting or publishing a system description paper?</summary>

There are no fees for submitting or publishing system description papers.
</details>

<details class="faq-item" markdown="1">
<summary>Where will SemEval-2027 take place?</summary>

SemEval-2027 will be co-located with ACL 2027 in Kyoto, Japan.
</details>

<details class="faq-item" markdown="1">
<summary>Is conference registration required for publication or presentation?</summary>

Registration is not required for publication. However, at least one author must register if the paper is to be presented at the workshop.
</details>
</section>

<section class="faq-section" id="support" markdown="1">
## Support

<details class="faq-item" markdown="1">
<summary>What if my research raises ethical concerns?</summary>

Discuss ethical considerations, including potential misuse, clearly in the system paper.
</details>

<details class="faq-item" markdown="1">
<summary>Should I release my code?</summary>

Code and data release is highly encouraged. Include the URL in the system paper when possible to support reproducibility.
</details>

<details class="faq-item" open markdown="1">
<summary>My question is not answered here. Who should I contact?</summary>

Ask in the [community Slack]({{ site.slack_url }}) or contact the [MMCultureQA organizers]({{ '/organizers/' | relative_url }}). Check official SemEval-2027 and ACL 2027 announcements for workshop and conference logistics.
</details>
</section>
